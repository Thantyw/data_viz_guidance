Bubble Chart — ACS data

U.S. Census Bureau, 2024 ACS 1-Year Estimates, Detailed Tables B19013 (income), B17001 (poverty counts and universe) and B01003 (total population). Poverty percentage = 100 × below-poverty count / poverty-status universe.

Bubble area, rather than radius, is proportional to population. Published income and poverty-count MOEs remain in the data table; no MOE was calculated for the derived poverty percentage. The companion scatter plot uses identical x/y values and equal marker areas. Four state observations do not establish causation.

Run completed November 10, 2026 (UTC). Readable cells round numeric values to two decimal places where needed; CSV downloads preserve exported precision. These tables have not completed screen-reader testing.
